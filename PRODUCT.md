# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Health-system CTOs, clinical-operations leaders, and digital-health founders evaluating a development partner. They arrive skeptical — burned by agencies that didn't understand HIPAA, FHIR, or clinical workflow — and they evaluate vendors the way they evaluate medical equipment: calmly, against specs.

## Product Purpose

TesaLab is a healthcare development platform: a team plus pre-hardened infrastructure that designs, builds, and runs clinical-grade software — from first prototype to FDA-grade product. The site's job is to make a careful technical buyer think "these people are exact," and book a working session.

## Brand Personality

Exact, calm, engineered. The register of a calibrated patient monitor or a Swiss surgical-instrument catalog: deep cobalt fields, precise white type, and a single live ECG-green trace. Confidence through precision, never through volume.

## Anti-references

- Generic AI-generated landing pages: gradient text, glassmorphism, uppercase eyebrow labels over every section, identical icon-card grids, hero stat trios.
- Hospital-brochure warmth: stock photos of smiling doctors, teal-and-white "healthcare template" palettes.
- Crypto/SaaS maximalism: neon glows, bento grids, floating 3D blobs with no meaning.

## Design Principles

1. **Instrument, not brochure.** Every visual element should read like telemetry from a working system — the 3D scenes are signal visualizations, not decoration.
2. **Green means live.** ECG-trace green is reserved for live signals and the primary action. Nothing else gets it.
3. **One system of chrome.** Monitor-style readout labels ("TESA·MON") are the single named kicker system, used only on dark instrument panels — never as generic section eyebrows.
4. **Proof in sentences.** Metrics live inside prose and readouts, not in big-number/small-label stat blocks.
5. **Calm motion.** Choreographed, eased, and purposeful; each reveal fits what it reveals. Full reduced-motion fallbacks.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No formal certification target, but hold WCAG AA contrast (≥4.5:1 body text), keyboard-reachable interactive elements, and `prefers-reduced-motion` alternatives throughout.
